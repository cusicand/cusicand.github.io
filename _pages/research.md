topics:
  - title: Natural hazards
    # image: /images/research/your-image.jpg
    # caption: ""
    text: >-
      Currently, a central thread in my work is translating cryosphere signals into risk assessment,
      notably on the links between hazards and exposure. By linking glacier retreat, permafrost
      degradation and changing rock glacier kinematics to slope instability, outburst-flood pathways,
      and cascading geomorphic responses, I help identify hotspots where warming raises landslide and
      flood risk in high mountain environments.

  - title: Combination of optical and SAR remote sensing data
    # image: /images/research/your-image.jpg
    # caption: ""
    text: >-
      Methodologically, I fuse medium-resolution optical time series (e.g., Landsat feature tracking)
      with very-high-resolution photogrammetry (Pléiades/UAV) and SAR/InSAR to capture processes from
      annual to decadal scales. This integration improves velocity retrievals, elevation-change mapping,
      and deformation monitoring over debris-covered and periglacial terrain.

  - title: Mountain permafrost
    # image: /images/research/your-image.jpg
    # caption: ""
    text: >-
      During my PhD and subsequent research, I’ve specialized in mountain permafrost, more particularly
      in rock glacier dynamics and their climate sensitivity. I developed workflows to reconstruct
      multi-decadal velocities using a variety of sensors (aerial, Landsat, Sentinel-2), showing
      consistent, climate-linked kinematic trends across the Alps and Andes; this contributes to the
      emerging Rock Glacier Velocity (RGV) Essential Climate Variable framework.

  - title: Mountain glaciers
    image: /images/research/Zongo_BM-web.jpg
    image_full: /images/research/Zongo_BM.jpg
    caption: Zongo Glacier, Bolivia
    text: >-
      I began by monitoring mountain tropical glaciers to quantify how fast they are losing mass and
      what that means for downstream communities. My work has focused on the Central Andes (Peru,
      Bolivia, Chile) and the European Alps, combining historical and modern imagery to derive geodetic
      mass balance and validate with *in-situ* records. I worked a lot at Zongo Glacier (Bolivia) using
      Pléiades stereo photogrammetry and aerial photogrammetry.

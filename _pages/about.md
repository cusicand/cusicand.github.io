Hola! Hello! Salut! 

I'm Diego Cusicanqui, Research Engineer employed by the <u>Institut de Recherche pour le Développement</u> ([IRD](https://en.ird.fr/)) at the <u>Institut des Sciences de la Terre</u> ([ISTerre](https://www.isterre.fr/)). I'm a young Bolivian researcher who's self-motivated and has a strong background in photogrammetry and remote sensing techniques applied to glacial and periglacial environments. I also have expertise in computer science and data science. More details about my scientific pathway are in the [CV section](/cv/).

My research lies between glaciology and geomorphology, focusing on understanding the physical processes and controls of environmental variables on mass movements in mountainous areas. I use a combination of advanced remote sensing techniques (e.g. spaceborne, aerial and terrestrial photogrammetry and InSAR), fieldwork and spatial analysis to identify and investigate slope instability through the interpretation of surface deformation. I'm currently interested in periglacial environments, particularly in relation to the degradation of mountain permafrost in a changing environment.

Teaching is a big part of what I do in my research. I help students improve their analytical skills and get to grips with data analysis through hands-on experience. I've taught physical geography, geomorphology and remote sensing at different levels. I've also been a teaching assistant on international courses, including summer and winter school programmes.

I'll be uploading the material I use for my courses gradually. If you are interested in the material, please be patient &#128522;.
